# CancerCare — Step 3: Organization-style site

## Design direction

The public preview now reads like a community-focused nonprofit site: mission, planned focus areas, Embu County pilot, and a collaboration section. It retains the TypeScript English/Kiswahili switch and the disclosure that CancerCare is an early project concept.

The site does not claim NGO registration, existing partners, patient outcomes, donations, or live clinical services. Those details require real evidence and organizational decisions before publication.

## Visual asset

The hero uses a conceptual illustration at `public/community-care.webp`. It is not a photograph of CancerCare patients or staff. The image was generated with the built-in image tool, then resized and compressed for the site (about 108 KB).

Prompt used:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: conceptual hero artwork for a nonprofit-style CancerCare website. Create a warm, contemporary editorial illustration of three adult Kenyan community members in a supportive conversation about finding trustworthy healthcare information. One person holds a simple blank leaflet. They are in a shaded community courtyard with gentle greenery. Textured gouache and cut-paper feel, refined shapes, natural expressions, muted forest green, clay orange, warm cream, soft gold. Wide landscape composition, the people grouped toward the right and center with breathable negative space, suitable for a responsive website crop. Clearly an illustration rather than a photograph. No text, logos, medical symbols, charts, needles, hospital setting, or claims of an actual organization event. No identifiable real people.

## Next product step

Build the Embu County facility-directory structure and verify every listing and cancer-related service before treating it as a care option. The Kiswahili copy also needs fluent human review before public release.
