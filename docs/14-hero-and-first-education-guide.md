# Hero imagery and first education guide

The homepage hero uses the user-supplied mint background (`hero-mint-background.jpg`) and a transparent cutout derived from the user-supplied doctor-and-patient image (`hero-care-cutout.png`). The cutout was generated to remove a baked-in checkerboard background. It is labelled as an AI illustration because it does not depict actual CancerCare patients or staff.

The first cancer education section contains three general questions in English and Kiswahili. Its source is the [WHO cancer fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cancer), checked on 7 November 2026. It explains cancer, screening, and what a screening result can mean. It does not offer diagnosis, individual screening eligibility, or treatment recommendations.

The Kiswahili wording and all medical content still need review by qualified local healthcare professionals before a public clinical-information launch. The section displays that review status. The facility directory remains empty until provider verification is complete.
