# Screening guide preview

The bilingual screening guide is the first complete interactive learning journey. A visitor can choose among learning about screening, noticing a health change, or understanding a screening result. The selection stays in browser memory only; it is not stored or sent to a server.

The guide explains general concepts and gives questions to take to a health professional. It does not select a test, assess symptoms, interpret an individual's result, or recommend a facility. The directory link leads to the existing verification-progress section until provider-confirmed listings are available.

Sources checked on 7 November 2026:

- [Kenya Ministry of Health, National Cancer Screening and Early Diagnosis Guidelines, second edition (2024)](https://www.afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2025-03/National%20Cancer%20Screening%20Guidelines%202024.pdf), especially pages 3–5 on screening, early diagnosis, and follow-up.
- [WHO cancer fact sheet](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/cancer), early-detection section.

All patient-facing English and Kiswahili wording awaits review by qualified local healthcare professionals before a public clinical-information launch. Local speakers should also check the Kiswahili for clarity.
